01

The fund

To a fund, a company is a row.

Revenue, margin, headcount, a hold period. A partner sees forty of these. The row is true, and it explains almost nothing about where the time goes.

02

The deal

The data room is what a company chooses to show.

Decks, contracts, audited accounts. We add one thing: read-only access to the systems the company actually runs on, and a working demo on its own data before the committee meets. Diligence you can click.

03

The company

Inside, the business runs on paper.

Email threads twenty people long. Spreadsheets with six versions. A system from another decade that everyone has learned to work around. Nobody drew this. It grew. And it is where the value is.

04

The floor

This is where we sit.

Our engineers work from the client’s own desk: the back office, the plant, the fund’s Monday meeting. We read the systems, sit with the people who do the work, and build with them. One workflow at a time, in production, on their data, in weeks.

05

The sheet

One document at a time, the paper becomes a system.

A delivery note becomes a record. A quote request becomes a priced reply. A thread becomes a case with an owner. A person approves every result until the numbers match. Then we stay until it runs without us.

Ninety percent software. Ten percent AI.

The model is the easy part. The hard part is the hand-off, the exception, the writeback, and the person who has to trust it. Four rules keep us honest.

  • The human stays in the first version.

    The system proposes and shows its evidence. A person approves. Liability sits with the client, and the design respects that.

  • Cost per record is a design constraint.

    If a model call costs more than the person it helps, it is the wrong architecture. Classical methods do the bulk. Models do the residual.

  • Ground truth before pipelines.

    We rebuild the truth from source documents by hand before we trust a system with a corpus. Where it is not certain, it says so.

  • Your data stays in your jurisdiction.

    EU environments for EU companies. Encrypted deliverables, deletion policies, and access we can account for.

The same team, in all three places.

What we learn on the floor changes how we read a data room. What we build for one company becomes a pattern for the next. The fund sees the same measures across its portfolio, and the deal team sees what a company is really made of before it buys.

  • Before the deal. Diligence with working software as the output, not a slide on AI potential.
  • Inside the fund. Internal tools the fund’s own team uses every week, built inside its secure environment.
  • Inside the companies. Production systems on the shop floor and in the back office, with engineers on site until it runs.
