# Third-party assets

All models in this folder are from Kenney (https://kenney.nl), released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (public domain). Kits used: Furniture Kit, Factory Kit 3.0, City Kit (Industrial) 2.0, City Kit (Commercial) 2.1, Car Kit. Only the models the site uses are included; each textured kit keeps its own `Textures/colormap.png`.

The HDRI in `public/hdr/` (`moonless_golf_1k.hdr`) is from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), CC0 1.0.

Attribution is not required by CC0. It is given here because the work deserves it.
